A Few Words About Us
Serving Best Shalimar Restaurant And Steaks Since 2014
At Shalimar Restaurant, we believe that everyone should be able to experience the rich and diverse cuisine of India, without having to break the bank. That’s why we’ve made it our mission to offer high-quality dishes at wallet-friendly prices.
From classic favorites like butter chicken and biryani, to more adventurous dishes like vindaloo and paneer tikka masala, our menu has something for every palate. We use only the freshest ingredients and traditional cooking techniques to ensure that every dish is bursting with flavor.
So come and join us at Shalimar Restaurant, and experience the taste of India like never before. Our cozy restaurant and friendly staff are waiting to welcome you with open arms.
