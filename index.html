Our story

Restaurant Shalimar was started in 1990 by Mrs. Uma Nigam. Using what she learned growing up in Northern India, Uma was the first to bring Indian cuisine to the Sherbrooke region.

From our own garden

Every summer, vegetables and spices grow organically in our family garden in Canton-de-Hatley, then end up on your table.

Vegetarian and vegan

Lean cuts of meat, and a vegetarian menu as varied as India’s regions — with vegan options throughout.

The kitchen

In 1993 Jyoti Nigam left his work at CHUS Sherbrooke to join his mother full time. When Lizy Carvalho arrived from Mumbai in 1995, she brought the authentic dishes of her native Kerala — and our kitchen has kept refining its recipes for every taste ever since.
