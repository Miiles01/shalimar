Our story

Shalimar serves authentic Indian and Chinese cuisine at an honest price. Since 2014 we have believed that everyone should be able to enjoy the rich, diverse food of India — without having to break the bank.

Fresh every day

We use only the freshest ingredients, so every dish is bursting with flavour.

Traditional techniques

Tandoor, slow-cooked gravies and fresh breads, made the way they should be.
